Max Heermann

Welcome! I'm a political scientist studying the domestic politics and legitimacy of international cooperation in Europe and beyond.

Currently, I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center of International and Comparative Studies at ETH Zurich and an associated researcher at the Cluster of Excellence 'The Politics of Inequality' at the University of Konstanz. I received my PhD from the Department of Politics and Public Administration at the University of Konstanz in March 2024.

My research on European integration, international solidarity, and digital sovereignty has been published among other outlets in the British Journal of Political Science, European Journal of Political Research, Journal of European Public Policy, and European Union Politics.

In my free time, I enjoy hiking, skiing, and going to underground music gigs. I'm a passionate cinephile and long-term volunteer at (and former board member of) the independent Zebra Kino Konstanz, as well as a founding member of the SHIVERS Film Festival.
