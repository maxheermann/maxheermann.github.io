Max Heermann

Welcome! I'm a political scientist studying the domestic politics and legitimacy of international cooperation in Europe and beyond. Currently, I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center of International and Comparative Studies at ETH Zurich and an associated researcher at the Cluster of Excellence 'The Politics of Inequality' at the University of Konstanz. I received my PhD from the Department of Politics and Public Administration at the University of Konstanz in March 2024.

My research on European integration, international solidarity, and digital sovereignty has been published in the British Journal of Political Science, European Journal of Political Research, Journal of European Public Policy, European Union Politics, JCMS: Journal of Common Market Studies, Swiss Political Science Review, German Politics, and NPJ: Vaccines.

In my free time, I enjoy hiking, skiing, and going to underground music gigs. I'm a passionate cinephile and long-term volunteer (and former board member) of the independent Zebra Kino Konstanz, as well as a founding team member of the SHIVERS Film Festival.
